Three steps. No timeline.

You never open an editor. You paste a link and download what comes back.

  1. Paste the link

    Copy the GTA 6 stream link from the streamer you are watching and drop it in.

  2. Klipit finds the moments

    It scans the stream and picks the parts most likely to get shared: fails, clutch plays, rage and chaos.

  3. Download and post

    Each clip comes back vertical with a title, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
