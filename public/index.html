How it works

Three steps. Zero editing.

  1. Paste the link

    Copy the link of any stream, VOD or past broadcast and drop it in the box. Any format works.

  2. Klipit finds the moments

    It scans the stream and picks the parts most likely to get shared: fails, clutch plays, rage and chaos.

  3. Download and post

    Every clip comes back vertical with a title and caption, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Help

Quick answers.

Which links work?

YouTube, Twitch and Kick. Full links, short links like youtu.be, mobile links, Shorts, live replays, Twitch clips and channel links all work. You can even paste a whole sentence with the link in it.

How many clips do I get?

It depends on your plan: 3 per stream on Starter, 15 on Pro and 40 on Elite.

Do Twitch and Kick VODs without captions work?

Yes. When a VOD has no captions, Klipit listens to the audio, finds the biggest reactions, transcribes those moments and picks the best ones.

Can I clip a stream while it is live?

Paste the link once the stream ends and the VOD is up. Klipit clips finished streams and VODs.

How do I sign in on a new device?

Use the email on your plan and the last 4 digits of the card it bills. You can update your card, see invoices or cancel from Billing.

How do I pay and cancel?

Plans are billed monthly through Stripe. Cancel anytime from Billing after you sign in. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.
